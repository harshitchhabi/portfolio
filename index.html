02 / ABOUT ME

Who I Am

Hey! I'm Harshit. I'm a Computer Science student at VIT Vellore specializing in Information Security, with a CGPA of 9.00. I'm the kind of developer who genuinely enjoys the process: planning an architecture, writing clean code, debugging at 2 AM, and shipping something I'm proud of.

My technical interests span across the stack. I build full-stack web applications with React, Next.js, and Node.js. I work on AI and computer vision projects using PyTorch, MediaPipe, and FastAPI. I design cryptographic security systems with real encryption standards (AES-256, RSA-2048). And I enjoy working with databases and data analytics, structuring data in PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Firebase to meaningful insights.

Beyond coding, I've interned as a Cybersecurity Analyst at State Bank of India (Global IT Centre), where I analyzed enterprise security infrastructure and evaluated vulnerability landscapes. On campus, I serve as Technical Head at Dream Merchants, where I architected a real-time auction platform with Next.js, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. I'm also a Core Member at Bulls & Bears, contributing to fintech initiatives and student community projects.

I care about building things that are useful, well-designed, and secure by default and not just functional. Whether it's a transportation platform with real-time maps, an ASL translator with live camera feeds, or a file encryption pipeline with dual-layer security, I always aim for production-quality work.
